About

From food trucks to supply chains.

I started out in food. I studied integrated food studies, built and ran my own food truck, and managed EU research projects at a culinary school. Today I work with clinical trial supply in pharma, where systems, timelines and people all have to fit together for medicine to reach the right patient at the right time.

The thread through all of it is the same: I like taking something messy and making it work. A process nobody quite understands, a project with too many moving parts, a tool that almost does the job. I sketch it, simplify it and build the version that people actually want to use.

What I'm good at
  • 01Turning complex processes into clear, simple tools.
  • 02Bridging people, systems and data so projects actually move.
  • 03Getting from idea to something real, quickly and without fuss.

When I'm not building, I'm usually on a gravel bike, renovating an old house in Copenhagen or making coffee slightly too carefully.
