I started out in food. I studied integrated food studies, built and ran my own food truck, and later worked as a project manager at a culinary school, running EU-funded projects for vulnerable young people. Today I build and validate RTSM systems in pharma: the IRT software that randomises patients, keeps trials blinded and makes sure the right medicine reaches the right site at the right time. Everything under GxP, where every click is documented and nothing is left to chance.

The thread through all of it is the same: I like taking something messy and making it work. A process nobody quite understands, a project with too many moving parts, a tool that almost does the job. I sketch it, simplify it and build the version that people actually want to use.

What I'm good at
  • 01Turning complex processes into clear, simple tools.
  • 02Bridging people, systems and data so projects actually move.
  • 03Getting from idea to something real, quickly and without fuss.

I like having fun, so I make things that are fun. That is where the name comes from. Stillwork is my workbench in public. Some things here are finished, some are experiments, and some may grow into something bigger. Everything is built to be useful first and quiet second. When I'm not building, I'm usually on a gravel bike, renovating an old house in Copenhagen or making coffee slightly too carefully.
