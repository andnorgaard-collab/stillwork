Mark

Set in lowercase serif. The dot over the i is always ember, and it is the only colour the wordmark carries. The mark is the same dot with a hand-drawn ring around it, for small sizes such as the favicon.

Colour

Warm paper, soft ink and two accents with different volumes. Ember is the quiet one: a dot, an underline, a single word. Cobalt is the loud one, kept for the brush. Moss means done or live. Click a colour to copy it.

Two tools. The pen is ember and quiet: an underline, a ring, an arrow, drawn with real pressure so no two lines look alike. The brush is cobalt and loud: one broad stroke behind the word that matters, or a spark beside it, for the funny point or the thing you must not miss.

Rules of thumb

  • 01Useful first, quiet second.If a detail does not help someone do the thing, it goes.
  • 02Ember whispers, cobalt shouts.One ember detail per screen, and at most one cobalt brush moment per page. Both lose their meaning when they are everywhere.
  • 03Serif says it, sans explains it, mono labels it.Three voices, never more.
  • 04Hairlines and soft corners, no heavy shadows.Rules are one pixel. Surfaces round off at 14, controls are pills.
  • 05Motion is slow, small and optional.Things rise a few pixels and settle. Reduced-motion settings are always respected.
